Manas — the guide

Manas (मनस्, "the mind") — a study library, and beyond. Mind maps on your phone: ready-made libraries to study, and your own maps to build. Open it at https://md1secretary108.github.io/manas/ (any phone or computer) or install the Android app (Manas.apk from the same site). Made by Kewal Bhatt (Kaivalya Krishna Das), Nairobi. Version 7.20 · November 2026. What changed lately: see What's new at the end, or in the app: home menu (⋮) → What's new.

1. What Manas is

  • A mind map is one idea in the middle with branches of ideas around it. Each idea is a bubble. Tap a bubble to open its menu: its note and big buttons — open or fold its ideas, go inside, hear the moment, read the transcript, and ⋯ More.
  • Libraries are collections of ready-made maps, written in our own words, each bubble linked to its source (a recording at the exact minute, a verse, a guideline).
  • Your own maps are yours to build, edit, present and study.
  • Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Back up from the home menu.

3. First steps

  1. Open Manas. The first time, it asks "What would you like to study?" — pick one or more libraries.
  2. The home screen is your shelf: one card per library, and your own maps below.
  3. Tap a library → its series or books → a class or chapter → its map.
  4. Tap any bubble → a peek card shows the note and buttons:
  5. ▶ Hear this moment — the recording at that minute
  6. 📝 Read what Maharaj says — the whole class as text, scrolled to that passage and highlighted; read on before or after it
  7. 📖 Read the verse — the verse on vedabase.io
  8. 🔊 Read aloud — the phone reads the bubble and its note to you (tap ⏹ to stop)
  9. Back (button or phone's back key) returns where you came from. Take the tour again anytime: home menu (⋮) → Take the tour.

4. Finding your way

  • Contents — every library has a contents page like a book: groups, classes, main points in teaching order. Tap a line to jump straight there.
  • Doors — a bubble with a small arrow and a double ring opens another map. The "You are here" trail at the top shows the way back.
  • Search — the home search box finds words inside every map and takes you to the bubble. It ignores accents (Prahlāda = prahlada).
  • Topics — "Also in N other places" on a peek card shows every moment on that topic across all maps.
  • Read as list — any map as a simple indented list (toolbar or ⋯ menu).
  • Fold all / Open all — on a small phone, tap Fold all in the bottom bar (no bubble selected): only the main branches stay, like a summary. Tap + on one to open it, or Open all.
  • Home button — the house at the top takes you straight home from deep inside a library. Recent on the home screen reopens your last maps.
  • Press and hold a bubble for more: links, copy the words, open map, report a mistake.

5. Studying

  • Tap Study on any map (or one branch of it):
  • Flashcards — answer Didn't know / Almost / Knew it; cards come back just before you would forget.
  • Quiz — ten multiple-choice questions.
  • Guided lessons (Accessible learning — IGCSE): tap a question bubble and it opens full screen, one step at a time (Read the question → Find key words → Plan → Write → Check → Self-test), read aloud by your phone. ◀ Back / Next ▶ to move, 🗺️ See as a map to return, ▶ Start lesson on the bubble to open it again, Voice: on/off to stop the reading. In the Self-test, tap Show the answer after you have said yours.
  • Progress stays on your device and is included in backups.

6. 3D view (Orbit) and Stories

  • Tap Orbit to see the map in 3D. Tap a bubble to bring it to the front; bubbles sharing a topic glow and join up.
  • Stories (⋯ → Stories) walk through a map step by step; Present in 3D for a talk or class.

7. Making your own maps

  1. Home → New mind map.
  2. Tap a bubble, then use the buttons at the bottom to add an idea beside or under it.
  3. Double-tap to change the words. Drag the orange ring to move an idea under another.
  4. Add pictures (cartoons, drawings, photos), shapes, colours, tasks and due dates, arrows (Connect) and brackets (Merge).
  5. Map turns a bubble into a door to another map — build a map of maps.
  6. Undo / Redo are always at the top.
  7. Talk — tap Talk and say (or type) one idea at a time; with the AI helper, Manas suggests where each idea fits. Map from your words on the home screen turns a few sentences into a whole map.
  8. AI helper (optional, free) — home menu → AI helper: add your own Google AI key in three steps. Library maps are read-only; tap Copy to edit to make your own copy.

8. Your data and backups

  • Home menu → Back up all maps — share the small backup file to Drive, Gmail or WhatsApp. A reminder appears if you haven't backed up.
  • Restore brings maps and study progress back. Libraries can always be re-added.
  • Get updates by email (optional) — home menu; confirm by email; unsubscribe in one tap.
  • Under 18? On the first-time email screen, a parent or guardian enters their own email; nothing about the child is stored.
  • Privacy: home menu → Privacy and your data — what is sent with your email, why, where it is kept and how to have it deleted. Your maps never leave your device unless you share them.

9. Settings

  • Dark / light — the sun/moon button on the home screen, or home menu → Appearance.
  • Language — English or हिन्दी (beta): home menu → Language.
  • Install as an app — home menu → Install (Android prompt or iPhone "Add to Home Screen").

10. Help and feedback

  • Report a mistake on any library bubble or flashcard — speak or type, then Send to Manas.
  • Home menu → Send feedback (speak or type), or message Kewal.
  • Share with a class (⋯ menu) sends a link that opens the same map on students' phones; Print or save as PDF gives a clean outline.
  • About the notes: library notes are our own summaries. On Maharaj's maps, quotes are his exact words checked against the recording; the automatic transcripts are unverified, and doubtful moments are listed for review with Maharaj.
